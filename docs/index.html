Overview

  • Simulated cotton that hangs and moves on the body.
  • A tight, snug, good or loose result at chest, waist and hip, for each size in the shop's size chart.
  • Optional face photos give the avatar your skin tone and hair.
  • Everything runs on your computer. Your measurements and photos never leave your browser.

What it works with. Product pages that show a body-measurement size chart. It simulates t-shirts only.

How it works

  1. Enter your measurements

    Type your height, chest, waist, hip and shoulder width once. The extension builds an avatar with your shape.

  2. Press Read page on a shop

    Open a t-shirt, open the side panel and press Read page. The avatar puts on the shirt in the colour you picked.

  3. Compare the sizes

    Tap a size to see where it is tight, snug, good or loose on your body.

Your data stays on your computer

  • No analytics, no tracking and no ads.
  • It reads a shop page only when you press Read page.

Permissions

  • sidePanel
    Shows the try-on panel next to the shop page.
  • scripting
    Reads the product name, colour, material and size chart when you press Read page.
  • activeTab
    Lets Read page work on the tab you are on, without wider site access.
  • storage
    Keeps your measurements and optional face photos on your computer.
  • Site access (optional)Asked for the first time you press Read page on a shop, so the panel can read the page again when you change colour or size.
