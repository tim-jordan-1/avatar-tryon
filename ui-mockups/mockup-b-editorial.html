First, your measurements.

A soft tape and two minutes. We build your avatar from these, so the fit is yours, not a model's.

Body shape
How to measure
  • Chest: around the fullest part, tape level under the arms.
  • Waist: around your natural waist, above the belly button.
  • Shoulder width: across the back, from one shoulder point to the other.
  • Inseam: leave empty and we estimate it from your height.
